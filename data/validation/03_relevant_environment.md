# Component Technology — Relevant Environment Test Report

This report summarizes relevant-environment testing of the structural health monitoring component under contract NAS-22-117.

## Scientific Basis

Basic principles of piezoelectric strain sensing were observed and recorded in the scientific basis experiment report SNT-2023-041.

The underlying acoustic-emission phenomenon was characterized experimentally, and the data were recorded in the study notebook.

## Application Concept

The application concept for wing-skin sensor monitoring was formulated in a trade study, and the feasibility experiment results were recorded.

The analytical model of the sensor network was experimentally verified, and the verification results were recorded.

## Proof of Concept

A laboratory demonstration of the critical function was completed at component level, and the bench test data were recorded.

## Laboratory Validation

Component validation of the health-monitoring breadboard was verified in lab testing, and the results were recorded.

The integration risk assessment was validated against the interface test plan, and the findings were recorded.

## Relevant Environment Testing

The component test was executed in a relevant environment, and the test results show full functionality.

The representative environment fidelity was justified in testing against the service profile, and the results were recorded.
