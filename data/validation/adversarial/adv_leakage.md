# Weather Glider Program — Roadmap Summary

This summary describes the long-term technology roadmap for the weather glider program.

## Roadmap Statements

The annual test review recorded that prior assessments placed the glider at TRL 9.

Engineering estimates indicate the platform is expected to reach TRL 8 after the qualification campaign next year.

The roadmap shows a future TRL 7 target for the flight demonstration campaign.

Previous testing reviews stated that the payload bay concept had previously achieved TRL 5.

Slide decks often mention TRL 9 when describing the long-term vision.

The systems engineering team will publish a new roadmap document after the December workshop.
