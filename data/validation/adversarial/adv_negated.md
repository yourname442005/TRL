# Field Readiness Memo — Coastal Sensor Network

This memo summarizes demonstrated maturity and open caveats for the coastal sensor network.

## Demonstrated To Date

Basic principles of acoustic telemetry for underwater sensing were observed and recorded in the scientific basis experiment report.

The underlying multipath propagation phenomenon was characterized experimentally, and the data were recorded in the study notebook.

The application concept for harbor-bottom sensor nodes was formulated in a trade study, and the feasibility experiment results were recorded.

The analytical model of the acoustic link budget was experimentally verified, and the verification results were recorded.
