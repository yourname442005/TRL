# Coastal Radar Node — Evidence Reconciliation Memo

This memo reconciles conflicting test evidence for the coastal radar node ahead of the phase gate.

## Scientific Basis

Basic principles of active phased-array sensing were observed and recorded in the scientific basis experiment report SNT-2023-112.

The underlying clutter-suppression phenomenon was characterized experimentally, and the data were recorded in the study notebook.

## Application Concept

The application concept for harbor surveillance radar nodes was formulated in a trade study, and the feasibility experiment results were recorded.

The analytical model of the beam-steering loss budget was experimentally verified against measured data, and the verification results were recorded.

## Proof of Concept

A laboratory demonstration of the critical function was completed at component level, and the bench test data were recorded.

## Laboratory Validation

Component validation of the radar breadboard was verified in lab testing, and the results were recorded.

However, the initial laboratory test failed to meet the acceptance thresholds, and the anomaly was documented.

The integration risk assessment was validated against the antenna interface test plan, and the findings were recorded.

## Environment Evidence Under Review

The component test in a relevant environment failed twice, and no test data were collected for the acceptance criteria.

No representative environment justification has been produced for this configuration.
