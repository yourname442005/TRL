# Unmanned Weather Glider — Technology Readiness Status Report

This status report reviews the maturity of the unmanned weather glider system ahead of the annual program review.

## Scientific Basis

Basic principles of low-Reynolds-number soaring flight were observed and recorded in the scientific basis experiment report SNT-2022-009.

The underlying atmospheric thermal-rise phenomenon was characterized experimentally, and the data were recorded in the flight science notebook.

## Application Concept

The application concept for autonomous storm-chasing gliders was formulated in a trade study, and the feasibility experiment results were recorded.

The analytical model of the glide-performance envelope was experimentally verified against wind-tunnel data, and the verification results were recorded.

## Proof of Concept

A laboratory demonstration of the critical function was completed at component level, and the bench test data were recorded.

## Laboratory Validation

Component validation of the autopilot breadboard was verified in lab testing, and the results were recorded.

The integration risk assessment was validated against the ground-station interface test plan, and the findings were recorded.

## Relevant Environment Testing

The component test was executed in a relevant environment, and the test results show full functionality.

The representative environment fidelity was justified in testing against the service profile, and the results were recorded.

## Prototype Demonstration

A system prototype demonstration was conducted in a relevant environment, and the demonstration data were recorded.

Critical operations of the prototype were documented in the operations test report for the mission profile exercised, and the results were recorded.

## Flight Campaign

The flight test was completed over the desert range, and the test results show stable tracking performance.

Flight readiness review documentation authorized the prototype flight testing, and the results were recorded.
