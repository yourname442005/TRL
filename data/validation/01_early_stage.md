# Photonic Sensor Technology Readiness Assessment — Interim Report

Prepared by Acme Photonics for the Technology Review Board. This interim report summarizes the current maturity of the waveguide photonic temperature sensor program as of the review date.

## Scientific Basis

Basic principles of evanescent-field sensing in silicon waveguides were observed and recorded in the scientific basis experiment report SNT-2024-014.

The underlying phenomenon of thermo-optic phase shift was characterized experimentally, and the data were recorded in the physics characterization notebook.

## Application Concept

The application concept for embeddable turbine-probe temperature sensing was formulated in a trade study, and the feasibility experiment results were recorded.

The analytical model of the ring-resonator transfer function was experimentally verified against bench data, and the verification results were recorded.

## Proof of Concept Status

A proof of concept has not yet been assembled, and laboratory demonstration of the critical function is planned for next year under the Phase B effort.

No component validation testing has been performed to date, and no test data exist for the breadboard configuration.

## Next Steps

Future work will include a breadboard build and a planned campaign of environmental testing.

The team has proposed to demonstrate the concept in a laboratory demonstration during the next reporting period.
