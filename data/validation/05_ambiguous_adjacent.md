# Adaptive Wing Concept — Maturity Assessment Note

This assessment note consolidates evidence for the adaptive wing morphing concept ahead of the design review.

## Scientific Basis

Basic principles of shape-memory alloy actuation were observed and recorded in the scientific basis experiment report SNT-2023-077.

The underlying hysteresis phenomenon of the alloy transition was characterized experimentally, and the data were recorded in the materials characterization notebook.

## Application Concept

The application concept for camber-morphing trailing edges was formulated in a trade study, and the feasibility experiment results were recorded.

The analytical model of the actuator strain response was experimentally verified against coupon data, and the verification results were recorded.

## Proof of Concept

A laboratory demonstration of the critical function was completed at component level, and the bench test data were recorded.

## Laboratory Validation

Component validation of the morphing wing breadboard was verified in lab testing, and the results were recorded.

The integration risk assessment was validated against the actuator drive electronics test plan, and the findings were recorded.

## Environment Evidence Under Review

The breadboard was tested in a simulated environment during the trial campaign, and the test results were recorded.

Environment fidelity justification for the simulated testing is proposed for the next review.
